# Blackjack Game

## Description
This is a simple implementation of the classic card game **Blackjack** (also known as 21). The goal of the game is to have a hand value as close to 21 as possible without exceeding it. The game is built using **HTML**, **CSS**, and **JavaScript**.

---
You can play the game online by clicking [here](https://mikesirya.github.io/BlackJack-Card-Game/BlackJack.html).

## Rules of Blackjack

### Card Values
- **Number cards (2-10)**: Worth their face value.
- **Face cards (Jack, Queen, King)**: Worth 10.
- **Ace**: Can be worth 1 or 11, depending on what benefits the player.

### Objective
- The player and the computer (dealer) each start with two cards.
- The player can choose to **"hit"** (draw another card) or **"stand"** (stop drawing cards).
- The dealer must follow specific rules:
  - The dealer must **"hit"** if their hand value is less than 17.
  - The dealer must **"stand"** if their hand value is 17 or higher.

### Winning Conditions
- If the player's hand value exceeds 21, they **"bust"** and lose.
- If the dealer's hand value exceeds 21, the player wins.
- If neither busts, the hand with the value closest to 21 wins.
- If both hands have the same value, it's a **"push"** (tie).

---

## How to Play

1. **Start the Game**:
   - Click the **Play** button to start the game.
   - Two cards are dealt to the player and two cards to the computer (dealer).
   - The player's cards and total score are displayed.
   - The computer's first card is shown, but the second card is hidden.

2. **Player's Turn**:
   - Click **Continue** to **"hit"** and draw another card.
   - The new card is added to the player's hand, and their score is updated.
   - If the player's score exceeds 21, they bust, and the game ends.

3. **Stop the Game**:
   - Click **Stop** to **"stand"** and end your turn.
   - The computer (dealer) reveals their hidden card and starts drawing cards according to the rules.
   - The winner is determined based on the scores, and the result is displayed.

4. **Restart the Game**:
   - Click **Restart** to reset the game.
   - All cards are cleared, scores are reset to 0, and the game starts fresh.
